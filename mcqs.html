MCQ 1

Who is known as the Father of the Indian Constitution?

A. Mahatma Gandhi
B. Jawaharlal Nehru
C. Dr. B. R. Ambedkar
D. Sardar Patel

Answer: C. Dr. B. R. Ambedkar

MCQ 2

Article 21 deals with:

A. Equality before law
B. Freedom of speech
C. Right to life and personal liberty
D. Freedom of religion

Answer: C. Right to life and personal liberty

MCQ 3

How many Fundamental Duties are there at present?

A. 9
B. 10
C. 11
D. 12

Answer: C. 11

MCQ 4

Which Article is called the Heart and Soul of the Constitution?

A. 14
B. 19
C. 21
D. 32

Answer: D. Article 32

MCQ 5

Who appoints the Chief Justice of India?

A. Prime Minister
B. President of India
C. Parliament
D. Supreme Court

Answer: B. President of India
